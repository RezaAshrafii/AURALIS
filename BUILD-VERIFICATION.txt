Concrete fixes over v0.10.4
---------------------------
1. The Go probe no longer casts the WAVEFORMATEXTENSIBLE extension through a padded Go struct. Extension fields are parsed at Windows offsets 18/20/24.
2. Derived audio supports float32/float64/PCM16/PCM24/PCM32 and preserves all channels before safe mono derivation.
3. Unsupported/failed audio decode becomes visible telemetry instead of silently starving VAD.
4. UI shows encoding, RMS, threshold and voice state.
5. Polling cannot overlap; unchanged Health/Turn/Transcript DOM is not rebuilt.
6. Completed historical ledgers are not replayed on every launch.
7. The native probe is prewarmed in the background on Windows.
8. Session/capture startup latency is measured in the UI.
